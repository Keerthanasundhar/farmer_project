# Crop advisory guides

## Paddy
- Maintain standing water in early vegetative stages; avoid water stress during panicle initiation and flowering.
- If rainfall is frequent and field is saturated, avoid extra irrigation and monitor drainage to reduce root stress.
- If a heatwave is forecast, keep channels filled and plan early morning irrigation.
- Do not spray pesticides just before or during rain to avoid wash-off and poor efficacy.
- Source: TNAU Agritech, Tamil Nadu and standard rice agronomy guidance.

## Cotton
- Cotton benefits from regular moisture but is sensitive to prolonged waterlogging.
- Avoid spraying insecticides before heavy rain; choose early morning or late evening application windows.
- Monitor boll development closely during warm, dry periods and plan irrigation if soil moisture drops below moderate levels.
- Source: State agricultural university agronomy notes and integrated pest management guides.

## Banana
- Banana requires consistent moisture and protection from strong winds and flooding.
- Before a cyclone or heavy wind alert, secure plants, drain excess water, and avoid harvest during the storm window.
- Use mulch and drainage channels to reduce water logging after heavy rain.
- Source: Banana cultivation guidance from tNAU and national horticulture extension advice.

## Groundnut
- Groundnut should be harvested when pod maturity is reached and dry weather is expected.
- Preserve pods from wet weather to reduce fungal risk and avoid delayed harvesting during rain.
- Watch for moisture stress during pegging and pod development; irrigation support may be needed before flowering.
- Source: Groundnut agronomy recommendations and post-harvest handling guides.

## Generic advice
- Ask for location and crop if missing to improve local recommendations.
- State uncertainty in weather predictions and avoid if the forecast is unclear.
- Use local market prices and active weather warnings to decide on harvest or spray timing.
